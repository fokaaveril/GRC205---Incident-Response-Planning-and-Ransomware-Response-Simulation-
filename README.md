# GRC205---Incident-Response-Planning-and-Ransomware-Response-Simulation-
Incident response plan for a fictional ransomware attack on Northbridge Skills Institute: classification, CSIRT and RACI, playbook, evidence handling, communications and tabletop exercise.
This project is my submission for GRC105 (Incident Management and Business Continuity, Week 20) at the International Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics Academy (ICDFA). It responds to a fictional scenario in which Northbridge Skills Institute, a training provider with 1,800 learners and 90 staff, faces a suspected ransomware attack with possible data theft, 2 hours 15 minutes before an online examination.

What it covers

Incident classification: an evidence timeline that separates confirmed facts from hypotheses, severity rating, affected assets, and confidentiality, integrity and availability impact.
Incident response plan: policy outline, CSIRT structure, RACI matrix and a preparation checklist.
Response playbook: containment, eradication and recovery steps, an evidence register with chain of custody, and recovery acceptance criteria.
Communication and escalation: stakeholder matrix, an executive briefing, a learner notice and reporting obligations.
Tabletop exercise: a simulated walkthrough with a decision log and an improvement plan.
Case study reflection: lessons from a published healthcare ransomware case.

All organisations, systems and data are fictional. The exercise results are simulated, not real incidents. The tabletop and recovery times are planning proposals, and no real systems were accessed.

Frameworks referenced: NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3, the CISA #StopRansomware Guide and GDPR Article 33 (as an illustration only). The alignment is educational and claims no certification.
